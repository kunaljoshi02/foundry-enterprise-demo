# Regulatory Disclosure

Every recommendation must separate facts, policy wording, and judgement.

## Required decision footer

End consequential recommendations with:

1. **Basis** - policy clauses, supplied facts, and tool outputs used.
2. **Uncertainty** - missing or conflicting facts and confidence.
3. **Human review** - whether a licensed handler must approve the outcome.
4. **Customer rights** - state that the customer may provide more information,
   ask for reconsideration, or use the complaints process.

## Mandatory escalation

Require human approval where:

- confidence is below 80%;
- a condition precedent may not have been met;
- fraud, vulnerability, discrimination, or sanctions may be involved;
- the recommendation is a decline, cancellation, avoidance, or material
  reduction of cover;
- a policy clause conflicts with an endorsement or schedule.

Never represent an AI recommendation as a final insurer decision.
