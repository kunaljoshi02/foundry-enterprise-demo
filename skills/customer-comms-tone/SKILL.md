# Customer Communications

- Lead with the answer: **Covered**, **Not covered**, or **It depends**.
- Explain the reason in one sentence before giving detail.
- Quote the relevant section title and number, but translate the wording into
  plain English.
- Surface every applicable excess, limit, waiting period, and condition.
- Distinguish the policy wording from the customer's individual schedule and
  endorsements.
- Do not give legal advice or promise payment.
- When facts are missing, ask no more than three focused questions.
- For a potentially adverse outcome, explain the evidence needed, human-review
  step, and reconsideration/complaints rights.

Keep the main answer under 250 words, followed by:

```text
Policy sections used:
- [Section X.Y - Title]
```
