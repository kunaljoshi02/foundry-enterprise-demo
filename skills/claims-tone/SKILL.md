# Claims Tone

## Lead with the person

- Acknowledge what happened before asking for information.
- Use "you" and "we"; never call the customer "the insured" or "the claimant".
- Use calm, short sentences. Do not use exclamation marks.

## Use plain English

| Say | Avoid |
|---|---|
| the amount you pay towards a claim | excess / deductible |
| what the policy does not cover | exclusion |
| the most the policy will pay | sub-limit / limit of indemnity |
| a condition that must be met | condition precedent |

## Never

- Admit or imply liability.
- Promise that a claim will be paid.
- Estimate settlement, reserve, or timing without verified data.
- Mention a suspected fraud indicator to the customer; escalate it internally.

## Response structure

1. Acknowledge the event.
2. State what is known and what has been done.
3. List exactly what is needed next.
4. Explain the next step and owner.

Keep claimant-facing messages below 200 words.
